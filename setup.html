Start streaming in minutes

Follow our step-by-step guide to configure your device and connect to the Stream Nustel network.

1

Choose Your Subscription

Purchase a plan from our pricing page. After payment, you will instantly receive your login credentials (username, password, and server URL) via email and on your dashboard.

2

Download an App

Depending on your device, download a compatible IPTV application. We recommend TiviMate for Android TV, IPTV Smarters Pro for mobile and Samsung/LG TVs, or VLC Player for computers.

3

Enter Your Details

Open the app, select "Login with Xtream Codes API" (or similar), and enter the details provided in your welcome email:

  • Portal URL
  • Username
  • Password
4

Start Watching

Allow the app to load the channels, movies, and series list. This may take up to a minute on the very first load. Once done, you are ready to stream!

Troubleshooting & Quick Tips

Channels are freezing or buffering

Restart your router and device. Ensure your internet speed is at least 30 Mbps for HD. If using Wi-Fi, try connecting via Ethernet cable for absolute stability.

"Authorization Failed" error

Double-check your username and password for typos (they are case-sensitive). Ensure you are not connected to a VPN, as some servers may block specific VPN IPs.

App doesn't load the EPG (TV Guide)

Go to your app settings and look for the 'Update EPG' button. Sometimes the guide needs to be manually triggered on the first installation.

Need human assistance?

Our support team is available 24/7 to help you get connected. Don't hesitate to reach out if you're stuck on any step.
