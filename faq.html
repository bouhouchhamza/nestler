Activation & Setup

How fast is activation?

Usually within minutes after your payment is confirmed. You will receive an email with your credentials and setup instructions.

Do I need a VPN to use Stream Nustel?

A VPN is not required to use our service, as our connections are already secured. However, if your ISP blocks IPTV services, a VPN can help bypass those restrictions.

Plans & Payments

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from your dashboard. If you upgrade mid-cycle, you will only be charged the prorated difference.

What is the 7% top-up fee?

This is a small transaction and gateway processing fee applied by our secure payment providers to ensure your data remains protected.

Devices & Usage

How many devices can I use?

Depends on the plan you choose. Basic allows 1 concurrent device, Premium allows 2 devices, and Pro gives you access on up to 5+ simultaneous devices.

Can I use the service when traveling abroad?

Yes! Your account is not IP-locked to your home. You can stream from anywhere in the world as long as you have a stable internet connection.
